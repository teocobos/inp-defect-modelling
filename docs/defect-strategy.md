# Defect Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the strategy for selecting, generating and analysing native defects in crystalline InP.

## Initial Native Defect Set

The initial defect space includes:

### Vacancies

- `V_In`
- `V_P`

### Antisites

- `In_P`
- `P_In`

### Interstitials

- `In_i`
- `P_i`

## Vacancy Strategy

Vacancies should be generated by removing a single atom from a symmetry-inequivalent lattice site.

Symmetry analysis should be used to confirm whether multiple distinct lattice sites require separate calculations.

## Antisite Strategy

Antisites should be generated by replacing:

- P with In to form `In_P`
- In with P to form `P_In`

Local relaxation should be carefully monitored because antisites may produce substantial structural distortion.

## Interstitial Strategy

Interstitials require broader configurational sampling than vacancies or antisites.

Potential starting positions should include:

- high-symmetry interstitial sites
- tetrahedral-like sites
- bond-centred sites
- split-interstitial geometries
- configurations identified from literature

Multiple starting configurations should be generated systematically.

## Symmetry Reduction

Equivalent configurations should be removed using symmetry where possible.

The symmetry-analysis method and tolerance should be documented.

## Charge States

Candidate charge states should initially be guided by:

- nominal valence
- previous literature
- electron counting
- exploratory calculations

Production charge-state ranges should be expanded if unexpected stable states appear.

## Spin States

Potentially open-shell defects should be tested using spin-polarised calculations.

Multiple initial magnetic moments may be required.

## Defect Relaxation

Defect structures should be relaxed without imposing excessive symmetry constraints.

If a defect undergoes significant reconstruction, the final structure should be classified independently from the initial configuration.

## Metastable Configurations

Multiple local minima may exist.

Distinct relaxed minima should be retained if they differ meaningfully in:

- geometry
- electronic structure
- total energy
- charge localisation

## Defect Complexes

Defect complexes should not be introduced until the isolated native defects are characterised.

Later complexes may include:

- vacancy-interstitial pairs
- vacancy-antisite complexes
- impurity-vacancy complexes

## Defect Prioritisation

Priority should be based on:

- formation energy
- charge-state stability
- electronic activity
- mobility
- experimental relevance
- device relevance

## Required Outputs

For each production defect, aim to record:

- relaxed structure
- charge state
- spin state
- total energy
- formation energy
- structural distortion
- DOS/PDOS
- localisation
- CTLs where relevant
